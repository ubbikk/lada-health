# Review of the September 22 video using Gemini

The original ~28-second video was recorded just before upload, as confirmed by Dmytro. Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview processed the original video with audio; a second audio-only request checked the transcript without clinical context. This is an AI-assisted description, not a clinical examination. The root assistant visually inspected sampled frames but could not independently listen to the audio.

## Main findings

Both Gemini passes transcribe:

- Around 00:04: “Чувствую себя заторможенно.” — “I feel slowed down.”
- Around 00:07: “Мне даже клиентки сказали, что я очень медленно говорю.” — “Even my clients told me that I speak very slowly.”
- Near the end, she says she plans to sleep.

This adds a patient self-report, as transcribed by AI, and her account of others noticing slow speech. It does not establish the cause or an objective change from baseline.

Gemini describes intelligible speech in multi-word phrases and does not identify clear slurring, audible wheeze/cough/stridor, or loss of wakefulness in this short recording. It describes intermittent heavy/partly closed eyelids and purposeful gestures. Sampled visual review separately showed looking around, smiling, gesturing and lifting a cup. No walking is shown.

## Interpretation and limits

The useful distinction is reported slowing/tiredness versus clearly demonstrated slurred speech or inability to stay awake; the latter were not identified by Gemini in this clip. This is not proof they never occur. No baseline comparison is available. No oxygen, carbon dioxide, glucose or blood-pressure measurement can be obtained from these observations. No gait assessment is possible. The moving tight framing limits breathing assessment. The video does not resolve partner-observed unsteadiness or the reason for recurrent slowing.

The raw Gemini response overreaches in suggesting sentence completion establishes absence of acute severe respiratory failure. That inference is not accepted. Its normal-orientation/coordination implications and speculative medication examples are also not adopted as clinical findings. The prior recommendation for assessment while symptoms persist is unchanged.
