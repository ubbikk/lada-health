It separates reported events from possible explanations and ideas discussed but not yet tested. The broader medical and allergy history remains in [HEALTH_CONTEXT.md](../HEALTH_CONTEXT.md).

The pain’s location, exact delay after eating and severity have not been described for this latest episode. This is a partner report, not evidence that the crackers themselves are an allergen. ## Ideas discussed for restoring intake

These are **trial ideas, not results or a diagnosis**. They address the immediate problem of calories provoking pain while the cause remains uncertain.

1. **Start with small, repeated opportunities to eat or drink**, rather than trying to jump from near-zero intake to two full meals. A few spoonfuls or sips can test tolerance, followed by gradual increases if comfortable. Small portions must lead toward meaningful daily calories; remaining on tiny portions is not an adequate long-term solution. General hospital guidance for upper-gut meal symptoms supports smaller, more frequent meals and softer foods, but Lada has not been diagnosed with the conditions in those guides. [UCLH guidance](https://www.uclh.nhs.uk/patients-and-visitors/patient-information-pages/dietary-management-functional-dyspepsia-and-gastroparesis).
2. **Try smooth, soft or liquid forms of foods she currently accepts**: a nourishing smooth soup or purée, or an oral nutrition drink. Plain broth alone supplies little energy. Avoid making rice, oats or pasta default staples given her own history. Texture and portion size may help but cannot guarantee that eating will be painless. [UCLH guidance](https://www.uclh.nhs.uk/patients-and-visitors/patient-information-pages/dietary-management-functional-dyspepsia-and-gastroparesis).
3. **Compare the drinks by what matters to her.** Nutridrink Protein **Vanilla** provides **306 kcal, 18 g protein and no fibre per 125 mL**; it contains cow’s-milk protein, soy lecithin and flavouring. Nutridrink Compact **Fibre Vanilla** provides **300 kcal, 12 g protein and 4.5 g soluble fibre per 125 mL**; it also contains cow’s-milk protein and soy lecithin. Vanilla may be more appealing than neutral, but taste and tolerance are unknown. The fibre could add bloating or cramping during this flare, though her past obstruction alone does not prove the fibre product is unsafe now. [Protein Vanilla manufacturer information](https://nutricia.ua/product/nutridrink-proteyin-zi-smakom-vanili/), [Compact Fibre manufacturer information](https://nutricia.ua/product/nutridrink-kompakt-z-harchovymy-voloknamy-zi-smakom-vanili/). Nutricia advises sipping a bottle slowly over at least 30 minutes.
4. **Simple calorie powder is a different option:** Fantomalt is listed as maltodextrin only, at **384 kcal/100 g**. It can add carbohydrate calories to a tolerated drink or food but contains no protein or fat and is not a complete meal. It was listed in stock by one Ukrainian seller on 15 October; availability may change. [Ukrainian listing](https://daliaplus.com/products/fantomalt-fantomalt-400-g).

No trial of the ordered nutrition drink has yet been reported. Pain after crackers and earlier low intake mean a drink may also be difficult to tolerate; a few sips test that, rather than a full bottle at once. If repeated small trials still cause persistent pain, the food strategy is failing and the underlying cause and a way to deliver nutrition need reassessment. With **more than five days of little or no intake**, nutrition should be increased cautiously with attention to refeeding risk; September’s electrolytes do not establish present values. [NICE nutrition guidance](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg32/chapter/Recommendations).

## Information that would change the next decision

The most useful missing details are **where the pain is; how soon it starts after food or drink; whether it is burning, cramping or another pattern; how long it lasts; whether vomiting, distension or changes in stool/gas occur; what she can drink; and current weight/intake**. Recording one food or drink at a time with amount, time and subsequent symptoms would make a failed or successful trial interpretable. Whether she can tolerate even a few sips of the ordered Nutridrink Protein Vanilla is an immediate practical update. These details should guide the next food, medication or investigation decision rather than be treated as missing proof of a particular diagnosis.
